About me

I’m Jack (he/him), a 19-year-old business student from Massachusetts. I graduated from Montachusett Regional Vocational Technical School in 2026, where I studied Business Technology, served as class treasurer and class vice president, and was secretary of the National Honor Society.

In fall 2026 I started at UMass Amherst’s Isenberg School of Management, where I’m in the Dean’s Academy. I’m hoping to major in Operations and Information Management, with a double major in hospitality.

Outside class I’m usually behind a camera or planning a service project. I run Charming Memories Photography, directed multimedia for the MassSTAR youth leadership conference in 2025 and 2026, and was named the 2024–25 Massachusetts Leo of the Year by Lions Clubs International. Now I’m the Leo Advisor for Lions District 33A.

Why I do this

I like understanding why a decision gets made before I act on it, and I try to lead that way too.

Nothing I’m proud of happened alone. The Leo of the Year award and the MassSTAR recap videos both came out of teachers, mentors, friends and community members who believed in me, pushed me, and showed up when it counted.

I’m in college now, and the plan hasn’t changed: keep serving, do good work, and make the people who believed in me proud.
