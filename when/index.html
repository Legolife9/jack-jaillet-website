    How it works

    1. Make a poll. Pick the days and the hours you're considering. It takes about a minute.
    2. Send the link. Text it, email it, post it. People just type their name, no account.
    3. See the overlap. Everyone drags across the times they're free. The best times rise to the top, and you can lock one in and send it to everyone's calendar.

    Everyone sees the times in their own time zone. You can mark times as "if needed", and compare against your own calendar without it ever leaving your device.
