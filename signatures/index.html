    About Signature Studio

    A free, open-source email signature builder by Jack Jaillet. Design a signature, fill it in for a whole team, and copy it into Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail.

    Privacy. You don't need an account. Without one, everything you make stays in this browser and nothing you type is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Export backup to keep a copy.

    If you sign in, your signatures and companies are saved to your account so you can use them on any device. They're stored with Supabase (servers in the US), and only you can see them. Your email address is used only to sign you in. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time from Account. If you share a company, the people you add can see its signatures (and its employee details, if you allow that), and they see your email address as the owner.

    Analytics. Only if you accept the cookie banner, Google Analytics records which pages are visited. It never sees what you type, and private personal links are stripped before anything is sent. Change your answer any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the home screen. Details are in the privacy policy and terms.

    Images and icons. Images are used by link and never uploaded here. In a finished signature, icons are loaded from Iconify through images.weserv.nl, and cropped photos through images.weserv.nl. When someone opens your email, their mail app fetches those images from those services and your image hosts, which can see the request.

    Fair use. Only make signatures for yourself or for organizations you're allowed to represent.
