The short version

I graduated from MontyTech in June 2026 after four years in Business Technology, where I was Class Treasurer, Class Secretary and a member of the National Honor Society. Now I’m at Isenberg, major undeclared for now.

Outside class I take pictures for people: portraits, events, headshots and corporate work. I train people in bystander intervention with Training Active Bystanders, consult for Gathering Currents, and handle data for Bright Future Financial.

In 2025 Lions Clubs International named me the Massachusetts Leo of the Year.
