Privacy Policy

What this site collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: very little, nothing is sold, and analytics run only if you say yes.

Effective October 15, 2026.

Who I am

This website, jackjaillet.com, including Signature Studio, is run by me, Jack Jaillet, as an individual. I'm responsible for the personal information described here. For any question or request, email contact@jackjaillet.com.

When you visit any page

  • Hosting. The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address, browser details and the pages you request so it can deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Fonts. Most pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when the font files download. Signature Studio uses fonts stored on this site instead.
  • Settings saved in your browser. Your light or dark theme and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to me.

Analytics, only if you accept

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, Google Analytics sets cookies on this site (named _ga and _ga_ followed by an ID) and records the pages you view, the site that sent you here, your approximate location (city and country, worked out from your IP address, which Google Analytics doesn't store), your device, browser and screen size, and how long you stay. I use this only to see which pages are useful.

Google signals and ad personalization are switched off, and the data isn't used for advertising. Before a page address is sent, everything after the "?" is removed (except campaign tags such as utm_source), so private Signature Studio links are never recorded. Analytics data is deleted after 14 months.

If you click No thanks, Google Analytics doesn't load and no analytics cookies are set. You can change your answer at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; saying no later also deletes the cookies. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services (opens in new tab).

When you email me

Mail sent to contact@jackjaillet.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to my personal inbox. I receive your email address and whatever you write, and I use them only to reply to you. I don't add you to any mailing list.

Signature Studio

  • Without an account, everything you make stays in your browser's local storage. None of it is sent to me. Clearing your browser data deletes it, so use Export backup to keep a copy.
  • With an account, you sign in with Google. Google shares your email address, name, profile picture link and a Google account ID with Signature Studio. These are used only to create your account and show who is signed in. The signatures, companies and team details you save are stored in your account.
  • Where it's stored. Accounts are stored with Supabase, on servers in the United States. Each account can read and change only its own data, unless you share it.
  • Sharing a company. If you share a company, you give me the email addresses of the people you add. They can see the company's signatures (and its employee details, if you allow it), and they see your email address as the owner.
  • Personal links. A personal link lets one person see their own signature, and edit it if you allow that. Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with that person.
  • Other people's details. Team features let you enter employees' names, titles, email addresses, phone numbers and photo links. Please have their permission. I store this only for you and don't use it for anything else.
  • Images and icons. Images are used by link and never uploaded here. Cropped photos load through images.weserv.nl, and icons in a finished signature load from Iconify through images.weserv.nl. When someone opens an email with your signature, their email app fetches those images from those services and from the sites hosting your images, which can see that request.

Information from Google sign-in

Signature Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy (opens in new tab), including the Limited Use requirements. It asks Google only for your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture). That information is used only to sign you in, is never sold or used for advertising, and isn't shared with anyone except Supabase, which stores accounts. No one reads it unless you ask me for help or the law requires it.

What I don't do

I don't sell or rent personal information, I don't share it for advertising, and I don't build profiles of visitors. There are no ads on this site.

Services that handle data for this site

  • Cloudflare: hosting, security and email forwarding.
  • Google: Google Fonts; Google Analytics, only if you accept; Google sign-in for Signature Studio.
  • Supabase: Signature Studio accounts and saved data.
  • images.weserv.nl and Iconify: images and icons in signatures.

Each service handles data under its own privacy policy. I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the site.

How long it's kept

  • Signature Studio accounts: until you delete your account. Account > Delete account removes it and everything in it right away. Copies may remain in routine backups for a short time.
  • Analytics: 14 months.
  • Emails: as long as the conversation needs them. Ask and I'll delete yours.
  • Local storage: until you clear your browser data.

Your choices and rights

You can turn analytics on or off with Cookie settings, download your Signature Studio data with Export backup, and delete your account yourself. You can also email me to see the personal information I hold about you, correct it, get a copy, or have it deleted. I'll reply within 30 days.

In the EU, UK and similar places, I rely on your consent for analytics (which you can withdraw at any time), on providing the service you asked for to run your Signature Studio account, and on my legitimate interest in running and securing the site and answering email. You can also object to or restrict processing, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

In California and other US states with privacy laws, you have the right to know what I collect and to have it deleted. I don't sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and I won't treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and Signature Studio accounts are for people 13 and older (or older, where local law requires). If I learn that I've collected personal information from a child under 13, I'll delete it.

Security and location

Every page is served over HTTPS, Signature Studio doesn't store passwords, and its database only lets each account read its own rows. No system is perfectly secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security. Data is processed in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change significantly affects how your Signature Studio account data is used, I'll say so in Signature Studio before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact@jackjaillet.com. See also the Terms of Use.
