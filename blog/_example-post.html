Why I'm starting a blog

August 10, 2026 · 2 min read

A short note on why this page exists, and the kinds of things I plan to put here.

Most of what I do ends up somewhere as a single sentence: a line on a resume, a caption under a photo, a bullet on a timeline. That format is useful, but it leaves out everything that made the work interesting in the first place.

So this is the longer version. Somewhere to write about the projects I'm in the middle of, what a shoot or a conference weekend actually looks like from the inside, and the things I get wrong on the way to getting them right.

What I expect to write about

  • Photography, including the shots that didn't work and why.
  • Leadership and community work, and what running something teaches you that reading about it doesn't.
  • Whatever I'm learning at Isenberg that seems worth passing on.

No schedule, no promises about frequency. I'd rather post when there's something worth saying than fill space on a calendar.

Write the thing you wish someone had written for you a year ago.

That's the bar. If a post clears it, it goes up. If you want to talk about any of it, my inbox is open.
